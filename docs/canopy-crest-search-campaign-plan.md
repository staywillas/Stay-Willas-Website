## 3. Phone Call-Only Ad Setup Guide (Step-by-Step)

### Campaign Settings:
* **Campaign Goal**: Leads (or create campaign without a goal's guidance)
* **Campaign Type**: Search
* **Target Geographic Locations**:
  * Mumbai (All)
  * Navi Mumbai
  * Thane
  * Pune
* **Location Option**: Target people "Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations" (Avoid "Interest in" to prevent spam from outside states).
* **Bidding Strategy**: Start with **Maximize Clicks** (with a Max CPC bid limit of ₹25–₹40) or **Maximize Conversions** once call conversion tracking is verified.
* **Ad Schedule**: Set to active hours when your team can immediately pick up phone calls (e.g., Monday–Sunday, 08:30 AM – 10:30 PM).

### Call-Only Ad Creative Fields:
* **Phone Number**: Your official reservations number (e.g. `+91 91361 40555` / reservation cell)
* **Business Name**: Stay Willas
* **Headline 1 (30 chars)**: 4 BHK Villa For 16 In Khopoli
* **Headline 2 (30 chars)**: Call For Direct Dates & Rates
* **Description 1 (90 chars)**: Private 22ft pool, huge green lawn & fresh meals. Just 1 hr from Mumbai. Book direct & save!
* **Description 2 (90 chars)**: Ideal for family get-togethers & celebrations. Talk to our villa concierge right now.
* **Display URL**: staywillas.com / Canopy-Crest
* **Final URL**: https://www.staywillas.com/villa/canopy-crest

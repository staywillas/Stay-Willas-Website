### Validation recipe before spending

In Keyword Planner, use **Get search volume and forecasts**. Set Google Search, Mumbai/Navi Mumbai/Thane and Pune/Pimpri-Chinchwad, and inspect both the trailing 12 months and recent monthly trends. Include nearby feeder markets only if operationally useful.

For the niche shortlist, a suggested screening preference is **10–500 average monthly searches and Low/Medium advertiser competition**, then compare top-of-page bid ranges with acceptable economics. This is a selection rule, not a claim that these keywords meet it. A more searched feature term can remain useful if it generates profitable bookings. Kurvande-only phrases may be flagged low search volume and barely serve. [Keyword Planner](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243?hl=en), [low search volume](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2616014?hl=en).

Record: keyword, location, monthly searches, advertiser competition, low/high top-of-page bid, match type, planned destination, and validation date. Google forecasts—not search counts alone—are the appropriate place to inspect expected clicks at the intended budget. I cannot honestly supply measured monthly clicks or competition without that data.

## ₹500/day account structure

| Campaign | Average daily budget | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Willow Peak / Search / Nonbrand | ₹450 | Acquire Jacuzzi and A-frame cottage guests |
| Willow Peak / Search / Brand | ₹50 | Capture Willow Peak property-name searches |

Nonbrand has three ad groups: **Jacuzzi**, **A-frame**, **Local / couples**. Ad group spend is not separately guaranteed by this structure. Evaluate local terms as a smaller test; keep most attention on Jacuzzi/A-frame searches. Exclude Willow Peak brand queries from the nonbrand campaign so reporting stays distinct.

If brand receives essentially no eligible traffic, pause it and move its planned ₹50 to nonbrand. Do not force spending on terms with no demand.

- Campaign type: standard Search, using Google Search initially; exclude Display expansion and Search Partners for the first controlled test.
- Geography: target the guest's origin—Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad—not only the property locality.
- Location option: **Presence: people in or regularly in targeted locations**, to focus this small budget on local feeder markets. [Location controls](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9376662?hl=en).
- Match types: exact for proven/high-fit intents; selected phrase seeds to discover wording. Avoid broad match at launch for this limited test. Exact/phrase can still match close variants and related intent; review search terms. [Matching guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7478529?hl=en).
- Bidding: provisional **Maximize Clicks with a ₹20 maximum CPC bid limit** during the initial query-quality test. ₹20 is a proposed cost-control setting, not a verified local CPC. Revisit against Planner bids, delivery and qualified leads. If it prevents useful impressions, evaluate economics before increasing it. [Maximize Clicks](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6268626?hl=en).
- After tracking is trustworthy and lead quality is established, evaluate Maximize Conversions; do not optimize to page views or raw WhatsApp taps.
- Ads can serve any day for Monday–Thursday stays. Do not limit the offer's ads to Monday–Thursday: a guest might book a Wednesday stay on Sunday.
- Run website ads across the day if requests are accepted continuously. Schedule the call asset only during confirmed staffed hours; display the response expectation outside those hours.
- Start with English creative. Add language targeting/creative based on the languages the team can support and observed results.

At ₹500/day, a 14-day average-spend example is **₹7,000**, and a constant full-month budget corresponds to a **₹15,200 monthly advertising limit**. For most campaigns, Google can spend up to twice the average budget on a day, and the monthly limit uses 30.4×. Taxes/other account charges are separate. A ₹500 average daily budget is not a strict ₹500 daily cap. [Budget rules](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637?hl=en).

Illustration only, not a forecast: spending ₹500 at average CPC ₹10/₹20/₹30 buys about 50/25/17 clicks. Actual demand, auctions, quality and eligibility determine whether the budget spends and how many bookings result.

## Negative keywords

Start with irrelevant themes: **jobs, job, careers, recruitment, vacancy, vacancies, salary, internship, internships, for sale, property sale, long term rent, monthly rent, hostel, dormitory, day picnic, day use, water park, waterpark, camping tent, jacuzzi repair, jacuzzi installation, jacuzzi manufacturer, jacuzzi supplier**.

Add phrase negatives **"private pool", "swimming pool", "infinity pool"** for this Willow-only campaign, since that is a different product requirement. Review phrases carefully to avoid blocking relevant comparative searches unnecessarily.

Use exact negatives for pure informational queries such as **[lonavala weather]**, **[lonavala temperature]**, **[kurvande directions]**, **[kurvande map]** if they actually appear. Do not broadly exclude “map”, “reviews”, “price”, “booking”, “near me”, “couples” or “Jacuzzi”.

Negative keywords do not automatically cover all close variants; inspect singular/plural and spelling variations. [Negative keyword guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453981?hl=en).

## First 14 days

- **Before day 1:** validate keywords in Planner; fix location/pricing; verify tracking; confirm staffed response hours and actual availability.
- **Days 1–3:** check search terms, geo/device traffic, irrelevant queries, broken forms and actual team response. Exclude clear waste promptly.
- **Days 4–7:** compare qualified leads by intent. Add useful queries as exact keywords; remove irrelevant matches. Keep the small local test only if it delivers relevant inquiries.
- **Days 8–14:** compare dates requested, cottage availability, confirmed bookings and acquisition cost. Review conversion lag before declaring a keyword ineffective. Shift attention from cheap traffic to booked/qualified traffic.
- If a term spends about 1.5–2× the agreed acceptable qualified-lead cost without a qualified lead, review the query and landing page before pausing it. This is a provisional decision rule, not a universal benchmark; small samples and conversion lag matter.
- Set the acceptable acquisition cost from discounted booking contribution after operations and extras, rather than from room revenue alone. Do not increase budget until the team can identify which leads became bookings.
